### §1 — BRAND FOUNDATION: Who We Are 🔴 P0

**Mission Statement**

> To make the US F1 visa journey clear, connected, and community-powered — so every international student can navigate it with confidence.

**Vision Statement**

> A world where every international student has access to trusted, community-sourced visa guidance — regardless of their budget or background.

**Core Values — #FELG Aligned**

| Value | Meaning | #FELG Pillar |
|:------|:--------|:------------:|
| 🌍 **Global Community** | Built by and for international students worldwide — 100+ countries, one cooperative | 🫶 Giving |
| 🔒 **Trust & Accuracy** | Immigration is serious. Every piece of information is verified, sourced, and current | 📚 Learning |
| 💪 **Empowerment** | We don't just inform — we equip students to take confident action on their own behalf | 💰 Earning |
| 🫶 **Mutual Support** | Every approved visa becomes knowledge shared back to the community | 🫶 Giving |
| ♾️ **Cooperative Ownership** | Members own the platform. It exists for them — not shareholders | 🎉 Fun |

**Brand Personality (3 Words)**

> **Trustworthy · Supportive · Global**

**Brand as a Person**

> *The Supportive Guide* — A knowledgeable graduate student who successfully navigated the F1 visa process and now helps others. Excited about your journey. Honest about the challenges. Practical about next steps. Speaks with authority from lived experience — but never with arrogance, because they remember how scared they were at the start.

**Cooperative Ownership Statement**

> 🛂 F1visa.Net is a member-owned cooperative under the ♾️ WeOwnNet ecosystem. The brand belongs to the community — not to any individual or corporation. Brand decisions are made transparently, with community input, and documented openly.

**Tone of Voice Guide — #FELG Aligned**

| Axis | Position | Why | #FELG |
|:-----|:--------:|:----|:-----:|
| Formal ↔ Casual | 🟡 **Slightly casual** | Immigration is serious, but students need approachable guidance — not bureaucracy | 🎉 Fun |
| Serious ↔ Playful | 🟡 **Slightly serious** | High-stakes processes demand respect, but warmth and encouragement are essential | 🫶 Giving |
| Authoritative ↔ Friendly | 🟢 **Friendly-first** | Lead with support and community — earn authority through reliability and accuracy | 📚 Learning |
| Corporate ↔ Community | 🟢 **Community** | Speak as "we" and "our community," not "we at F1visa" | 💰 Earning |

**The Golden Rule**

> **Write like a trusted older student who has been through the process — not like a government agency, not like a marketing department.**

**Tone by Context**

| Context | Tone | Example | #FELG |
|:--------|:----:|:--------|:-----:|
| 📋 Visa instructions | Clear, authoritative, reassuring | "Here's exactly what you need for your F1 interview." | 📚 Learning |
| 🫂 Community forum | Warm, inclusive, welcoming | "Thanks for sharing your experience — this helps everyone!" | 🫶 Giving |
| 🎉 Success story | Celebratory, personal, proud | "🎉 Meet Maria from Brazil — approved for UCLA!" | 🎉 Fun |
| ⚠️ Error / denial support | Empathetic, practical, hopeful | "This is tough. Here's what to do next. You've got options." | 🫶 Giving |
| 📢 Marketing / social | Energetic, inviting, punchy | "Your F1 Journey Starts Here. Together. →" | 💰 Earning |
| ⚖️ Legal / disclaimers | Precise, neutral, compliant | "This information is for educational purposes. Consult an immigration attorney for your specific case." | 📚 Learning |

**Words to Use / Words to Avoid**

| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
|:-------|:---------|
| Navigate, journey, community | Leverage, optimize, scale |
| Together, we, our | "We at F1visa" (corporate tone) |
| Verified, trusted, sourced | "Guaranteed," "100%" (can't promise visa approval) |
| Students, community members | Users, customers, end-users |
| Cooperative, member-owned | Platform, product, SaaS |

### §3 — LOGO & WORDMARK 🔴 P0

| Asset | Format | Usage |
|:------|:------:|:------|
| **Primary Logo** — full color | SVG / PNG | Website header, print materials, social banners |
| **Secondary Logo** — icon-only (🛂 passport mark) | SVG / PNG | Favicon, app icon, social media avatar |
| **Horizontal Lockup** — icon + wordmark inline | SVG / PNG | Blog headers, email signatures, partner co-branding |
| **Reversed Logo** — white (for dark backgrounds) | SVG / PNG | Dark mode UI, dark social covers |

**Clear Space**
- Minimum clear space = height of the "F" in F1visa on all sides
- No other elements inside the clear space zone

**Minimum Size**
- Digital: 32px width (icon-only), 120px width (full logo)
- Print: 0.5" / 12mm width (full logo)

**❌ Never Do**

| Violation | Example |
|:----------|:--------|
| ❌ Don't stretch or distort | Maintain aspect ratio |
| ❌ Don't recolor | Use only approved colors |
| ❌ Don't rotate | Keep horizontal or as approved |
| ❌ Don't add effects | No drop shadows, gradients (beyond standard), or outlines |
| ❌ Don't place on busy backgrounds | Ensure minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1 |

**File Formats Available**

| Format | Use |
|:-------|:----|
| SVG | Web, digital — scalable |
| PNG (transparent) | General purpose |
| PNG (white bg) | Email, documents |
| WebP | Web (modern browsers) |

**Accessibility**
- All text/background color combinations must meet **WCAG 2.1 AA** minimum:
  - Normal text (<18pt): **4.5:1 contrast ratio**
  - Large text (≥18pt bold or ≥24pt): **3:1 contrast ratio**
  - UI components and graphical objects: **3:1 contrast ratio**
- Preferred combination: Trust Purple `#6C3BAA` on White `#FFFFFF` = ✅ AA pass
